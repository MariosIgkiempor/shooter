Separation force

Ticket: Separation force design on the Enemy behaviours map. Question: when several enemies of the same Movement Style chase the player, they currently beeline to the same point and stack on top of each other. This prototype adds a steering force that pushes same-style neighbours apart, blended with the chase direction. Tune radius, strength, and check whether brute-force neighbour checks hold up as enemy count grows past the real game's MAX_ENEMIES :: 24.

Chase-to-player here is a straight line (this prototype isn't testing the BFS pathfinder, only the separation force blended on top of a chase direction — in the real game that direction comes from chase_to's next waypoint instead).
