Off-screen spawn placement

Question this demo explores: when an enemy spawns, can we always place it outside the camera's visible rect — even while the camera is actively panning to follow the player — without landing it inside a wall or off the edge of the map? Move the player around, spawn enemies, and watch whether the chosen point (yellow) ever ends up inside the blue camera rect once it updates.
