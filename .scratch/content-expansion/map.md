# Content expansion

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A spec for a content expansion across four axes — **enemy types**, **Maps as an ordered difficulty ladder**, **weapons**, and **bosses** — closing every design decision so that the content model, the named rosters, and the machinery each axis needs are fully specified. Reaching the end of this map means nothing is left to decide before someone goes and authors the content; implementation itself is a separate follow-on.

The game is mechanically dense and content-thin. Every system needed to carry variety already exists and is fully specified — the Movement/Attack axes, the Spawn Trigger timeline, the Weapon tier ladder, the Shop, Account progression, Relics, and `Map` with its objective fields — but almost nothing has been authored into them: one Map, one `Enemy_Kind`, and eight `Weapon_Kind`s of which two are labelled placeholders in their own source comment.

## Notes

- **Decisions only.** Wayfinder's plan-don't-do default stands: tickets close decisions, they do not author content or land code. The pull to just start writing enemy presets is the signal the map has reached its edge.
- Domain: see `CONTEXT.md`'s `## Language` section. Grilling tickets call the Skill tool twice ("grilling" + "domain-modeling"); prototype tickets call "prototype".
- **This effort may redraw another map's scope boundary.** [Enemy behaviours](../enemy-behaviours/map.md) explicitly ruled *Attack Style expansion* out of scope ("this effort only generalizes movement; attack logic stays frozen"). [Enemy variety model](issues/01-enemy-variety-model.md) may reopen it. If it does, **append an amendment pointer** into that map's Decisions-so-far rather than silently overriding it — the precedent is [Art revamp](../art-revamp/map.md), which reopened weapon-action-feel's locked "new art assumed needed" decision exactly this way.
- **Three foreign fog items graduate here.** Clear each from its own map as the corresponding ticket resolves:
  - [Enemy behaviours](../enemy-behaviours/map.md) — "Spawner content authoring — which levels/spawners actually place Floater and Swarmer enemies, and in what mix" → [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md).
  - [Shop and upgrades](../shop-and-upgrades/map.md) — "Concrete named Melee tier-ladder content and Magic tier-ladder numeric stats" → [Weapon catalog expansion](issues/08-weapon-catalog-expansion.md).
  - [Account progression](../account-progression/map.md) — per-`Enemy_Kind` Gold values, marked "placeholder, content-authoring" in `enemy_gold_presets` → [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md).
- **The three-shape actor vocabulary is superseded — do not design against it.** [Art revamp](../art-revamp/map.md) ticket 01's rectangle/circle/triangle-per-Movement-Style rule was overtaken by commit `665ad38`, which unified every enemy into a square sized by `enemy_body_size(max_health)`, tinted per kind and faded by remaining health, and deleted `Actor_Shape_Kind`. The live identity vocabulary for enemies is **colour, size and opacity**; there is no shape budget to run out of. Corrected here and on [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md) after [Enemy variety model](issues/01-enemy-variety-model.md) checked it against the code.
- **Flat-shape tiles are already decided, not open.** [Art revamp](../art-revamp/map.md)'s [tilemap ticket](../art-revamp/issues/04-tilemap-shape-treatment.md) locked flat fill for floor and wall, a darker inset bevel on walls, wall-vs-floor read from `Tile.collides`, and `Map`/persistence untouched. [Per-map theming](issues/07-per-map-theming-and-ambient-effects.md) builds *on* that; it does not reopen it.
- **`map_icon_colors`'s own comment anticipates this effort**: "Presentation only, so it lives here rather than on Map itself... Promote it onto Map if maps ever gain real per-map theming" ([map.odin](../../map.odin)). It also warns what promotion costs — the editor would need a colour picker, and the generated `maps.odin` is rewritten every build.
- ~~**`MAX_SEARCH_NODES :: 1024`** caps a BFS against a map of 2592 cells, so a path spanning the *existing* map already fails~~ **Superseded by [Enemy pathing at swarm scale](issues/11-enemy-pathing-at-swarm-scale.md)**: the constant is *deleted*, not raised — one flow field flooded from the player covers every cell at once, and an unfilled cell means unreachable rather than out of budget. That resolution also found what the constant was quietly propping up: absent cells count as walkable, so nothing bounded the world at all. Original note: `MAX_SEARCH_NODES :: 1024` caps a BFS against a map of 2592 cells, so a path spanning the *existing* map already fails — `find_path` returns not-ok, `enemy.path` empties, and the enemy straight-line chases into walls. Found while resolving [Map layout authoring model](issues/02-map-layout-authoring-model.md); it is why map footprint is held flat, and the bug itself now goes to [Enemy pathing at swarm scale](issues/11-enemy-pathing-at-swarm-scale.md) rather than the sweep — at 150-250 bodies it stopped being a stray bug and became the deciding constraint on how enemies move at all.
- ~~**`MAX_ENEMIES :: 24`** silently truncates a composition batch.~~ **Superseded by [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md)**: the cap rises to **4096** and stops being a design input — it is a safety rail against a runaway `Repeating` trigger, not a budget the ladder is authored against. Rung 4 is authored at **150-250 concurrent**, a number set by screen legibility rather than by an array bound. What actually binds at that scale is per-enemy BFS, now [Enemy pathing at swarm scale](issues/11-enemy-pathing-at-swarm-scale.md). Original note, for the arguments that leaned on it: A deeper roster, denser ladder rungs, and a boss spawning adds all press on this ceiling from different directions — [Map ladder shape](issues/03-map-ladder-shape.md) named rung 4 ("Ring", swarm density at peak trigger overlap) as the concrete case that hits it first — and it is the reason [ADR-0017](../../docs/adr/0017-run-outcome-and-map-objective.md) derives Cleared from the timeline rather than a kill quota.
- **Persistence is a trap, not a formality.** ~~Any new `Movement_Style`/`Attack_Style` union variant needs its `*_Save` mirror with an explicit `kind` discriminant.~~ **Amended by [Enemy variety model](issues/01-enemy-variety-model.md)**: collapsing a composition entry to `(kind, count)` removed enemy unions from persistence entirely, so a new axis variant — including the Tell-carrying `Attack_Style` from [Boss model](issues/05-boss-model.md) — needs no mirror at all. `Spawn_Trigger`'s own condition/mode unions still do. `core:encoding/json`'s union-variant guessing is confirmed broken here — a `Kills_Reached`/`Repeating` trigger round-tripped back as `Time_Elapsed(0)`/`One_Shot`, silently wrong ([enemy.odin](../../enemy.odin)).
- **[ADR-0018](../../docs/adr/0018-weapon-visual-identity-is-per-kind-not-per-family.md)** governs any new weapon: per-kind geometry authored once in unit space, derived from `kind` at draw time and never stored on `Weapon` (it serializes into the save file), with distinctions that are **countable** rather than proportional.
- **[ADR-0017](../../docs/adr/0017-run-outcome-and-map-objective.md)** governs any boss: Cleared is the timeline exhausting *and* no enemies alive. A boss must be expressible in that frame, or the frame changes deliberately.
- Existing precedent worth reading before ticket 01: [ADR-0001](../../docs/adr/0001-weapon-wrapper-struct.md) (bare union vs. wrapper struct), [enemy-behaviours ticket 01](../enemy-behaviours/issues/01-enemy-data-structure-shape.md) (its two-axis extension), and `weapon_presets` (the flat-enum-into-preset-table counter-precedent).

## Decisions so far

- [Enemy variety model](issues/01-enemy-variety-model.md): `Enemy_Kind` becomes the authored unit, keying an `Enemy_Preset` table (movement, attack, `max_health`, colour, Gold — absorbing `enemy_gold_presets`), and a Map's composition entry collapses to `(kind, count)`. The behavioural axes stay open too, with an explicit rule: *behaviour* on the axes, *identity* on the kind. Attack Style is reopened (amending [Enemy behaviours](../enemy-behaviours/map.md)); `Charger` is the named Movement Style candidate. Deleting the templates from the composition entry removes enemy unions from persistence entirely, taking `Movement_Style_Save`/`Attack_Style_Save` and `map-builder`'s literal writers with them. `ENEMY_MAX_HEALTH` is deleted; no per-rung health scaling. Tuning is code-only, so the editor's composition UI collapses rather than grows. [ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-enemy-kind-is-the-authored-unit.md).
- [Map layout authoring model](issues/02-map-layout-authoring-model.md): layouts stay **hand-authored** — one drawn file per rung, generation and hybrid chunk assembly both rejected. The cost premise was stale: nothing reads `Tile.atlas_coords` since the art revamp made tiles flat fill off `collides`, so drawing a map is now blocking out rectangles. `atlas_coords` and the editor's tileset palette are deleted; the editor instead grows map-level authoring (New map, `player_start`, `time_limit`, `victory_multiplier`), which it has none of today. Footprint is held flat near ~54×48 because `MAX_SEARCH_NODES :: 1024` already can't span 2592 cells. Map validity (connectivity, start on floor, reachable spawn ring, extent bound) becomes a test over the baked `maps` table. A Map is a fixed place with a variable encounter — no Run seed. [ADR-0021](../../docs/adr/0021-map-layouts-are-hand-authored-places.md).
- [Map ladder shape](issues/03-map-ladder-shape.md): **five rungs**, ordered by an authored `rung` field on `Map`, each gated by clearing the rung below (rung 1 always open). `unlock_level` was rejected — Level measures banked Gold, so Fortune would compound into ladder access. The Account gains a **cleared set** (one bool per Map, persisted by identity string, not enum ordinal, since `Map_Name` is generated in filename order). A Run stays **one rung attempt**; the gauntlet reading was rejected. The curve is carried by roster and Spawn Trigger density, with layout hostility secondary; footprint, Run length and enemy health stay flat. `time_limit` is **mop-up slack** (`timeline_end + slack`), not a difficulty axis — a tighter limit makes a rung unwinnable, not hard. `victory_multiplier` rises shallowly (~1.5→2.5) because gross income already climbs; failing still banks at face value, so reliability is what keeps lower rungs worth playing. Rung 5 is the boss rung and its geometry is deliberately **legible** — escalation stops there. [ADR-0022](../../docs/adr/0022-map-rungs-are-gated-by-clears.md).
- [Boss model](issues/05-boss-model.md): the boss is an **`Enemy_Kind` in the ordinary enemy pool**, not its own type — so killing it *is* the last enemy dying and [ADR-0017](../../docs/adr/0017-run-outcome-and-map-objective.md)'s Cleared needs no change. **Three phases on health thresholds**, changing the attack rotation and nothing else: `Movement_Style` is fixed for life, and **adds come from the timeline, never from the boss**, so the timeline stays the only answer to "can more enemies arrive". Attacks go through a new `Attack_Style` variant carrying a **Tell** — the enemy-side telegraph, timed in **absolute seconds** rather than as a fraction, because the `action_rate` invariant [ADR-0004](../../docs/adr/0004-windup-fraction-not-duration.md) protects has no enemy-side existence and a learned reaction time must stay stable; the committed-action rule carries over. Shared with ordinary kinds, spending one of ticket 01's variant slots. Three facts the ticket assumed were stale: Windup is weapon-only, enemies have had **no** health indicator since `665ad38` (so the boss's restored one is not an ADR-0011 exception), and `enemy_body_size` clamps at 48px — so `ENEMY_SIZE_MAX` rises with the derivation untouched. Boss spawns from its own `One_Shot` at t=0 with a reserved `MAX_ENEMIES` slot, drops guaranteed Gold, and paths against a size-derived inflation radius. [ADR-0023](../../docs/adr/0023-enemy-tells-are-absolute-durations.md).
- [Boss telegraph and phase feel](issues/06-boss-telegraph-and-phase-feel.md): a **Tell shows as the claimed ground plus a body flash** — the floor says *where*, the body says *when*, and body-only forms were rejected because at 55px of escape "something is coming" is not actionable. The zone draws at **full extent from the first frame** and fills as the Tell runs. **0.55s** is the reference for a committed area attack, **authored per attack rather than per Enemy Kind**, so the variant holds a rotation of *(attack, tell seconds)* pairs. A Tell belongs to **committed area attacks only** — the volley control read as noise, so ordinary `Ranged` stays untelegraphed and the reopened `Attack_Style` slot narrows to "an attack that claims ground". The area **locks its bearing at Tell start** (closing [ADR-0023](../../docs/adr/0023-enemy-tells-are-absolute-durations.md)'s deferred [ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-ground-targeted-casts-lock-at-trigger.md) question) and the enemy plants for its whole duration. **Screen shake on resolve**, hit or miss. **Phases are legible on the Resource indicator alone** — no palette, freeze or burst — because [Boss model](issues/05-boss-model.md) missed a third cue that comes free: **pacing**, since a phase tightens recovery as well as changing the rotation. Prototype on branch `prototype/boss-tell`. Amends [ADR-0023](../../docs/adr/0023-enemy-tells-are-absolute-durations.md).
- [Per-map theming and ambient effects](issues/07-per-map-theming-and-ambient-effects.md): a Map authors **a floor colour, a wall colour and its set of ambient effects**, on `Map` itself rather than in a table beside it — `map_icon_colors` is deleted and the Map Selection swatch **derives from the wall colour**, so the menu cannot advertise a colour the world lacks. Its own comment argued the other way and both halves went stale: the editor is growing a map-level panel regardless, and a "colour picker" is six `ui.slider` rows. What decided it is the alternative's failure mode — a code-side `[Map_Name]Theme` literal gives a sixth map file a transparent floor with nothing to catch it. One authored colour puts the floor on the wall's hue ramp; a fourth buys an accent nothing asked for. **Colour alone does not carry five Maps**, so the ambient set is authored per Map rather than fixed-and-tinted: motes, off-grid floor patches, and a directional light wash. **No vignette** — the screen edges are where enemies enter, establishing that an ambient effect carries no information and must never occlude any. Floor patches are a decal layer, so they leave the locked per-tile-colour-noise rule intact, and tile rendering is untouched. Ambience keeps its own budget and two z-slots, never `game.particles`. Prototype on branch `prototype/map-theming`. [ADR-0024](../../docs/adr/0024-a-map-owns-its-visual-identity.md).
- [Elite and affix tier](issues/10-elite-and-affix-tier.md): **no elite tier and no affix layer** — a heavy enemy is an ordinary `Enemy_Kind` with more health, and **nothing modifies an enemy after the stamp** (no affix, no per-rung scaling, no aura, no Run-wide multiplier), now written into `CONTEXT.md`'s **Enemy Kind** entry rather than left buried in ADR reasoning. The affix died on a harder ground than the ticket expected: all three identity channels are spent — colour on the kind, size *derived* from `max_health`, opacity on the health fade — so an affix has nowhere to mark itself, and borrowing size makes the body physically grow, contradicting the base kind's silhouette. It also has no authoring site, since `(kind, count)` is exactly the narrowing [ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-enemy-kind-is-the-authored-unit.md) is. What the tier was wanted for survives as roster entries: `MAX_ENEMIES :: 24` caps rung 4 at peak concurrency, so above the cap pressure means heavier bodies, and a Tell-carrier plants for its whole Tell. Heavy kinds cap at **~136 health / 48px**, because one shared radius-1 inflated collision map serves every enemy and the boss's own second map is cheap only at one boss. They may not break the hue convention, pay their base rate through the ordinary 1-in-12 roll, and **"Elite" gets no glossary entry** — a term with no mechanism is how the affix returns under a new name. Amends [ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-enemy-kind-is-the-authored-unit.md).
- [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md): **nine kinds across six of fifteen (movement x attack) cells** — Grunt, Spitter, Wraith, Lancer, Sentry, Breaker, Mite, Gazer and the Warden — each debuting on one rung and thinning as later rungs add their own, filling [Map ladder shape](issues/03-map-ladder-shape.md)'s briefs. The premise it found first: **nothing on the roster could catch the player** (100 move speed against enemies at 30-50, melee at 10px contact), so kiting was unconditionally free on all five rungs. The rule now is sustained speed under 70, with **`Charger`** — the one new Movement Style — the only thing exceeding it, and only along a locked line for a bounded dash. The dash reuses the **Tell**'s vocabulary with lane geometry instead of a patch, spending no `Attack_Style` slot; **the second slot is banked**, since fifteen cells is already far more than a nine-entry roster exhausts. Five movement families forced an **evenly-spaced repalette** (Grounded green, Swarmer yellow, Floater violet, Charger red, Inert cyan), which in turn means **the boss's licence to break the hue convention is a licence on *value*** — the Warden is near-white, because no hue is left. Payout anchors at **0.6 x `max_health`** with three named deviations, and `base_gold` x `gold_multiplier` collapses to one field. Two findings outgrew the roster: **`Swarmer` is broken** — the only style that both collides and does not path, seeking a ring slot in a straight line, with slots placed by trigonometry that drops them inside walls — and **`MAX_ENEMIES` was never the ceiling**. The cap rises to 4096, rung 4 is authored at 150-250 concurrent, and what actually binds is per-enemy BFS, graduated to [Enemy pathing at swarm scale](issues/11-enemy-pathing-at-swarm-scale.md). That density also broke an economic assumption: Gold rolls per body, so Mite drops to **3 Gold** to keep [Map ladder shape](issues/03-map-ladder-shape.md)'s shallow `victory_multiplier` honest. Amends [ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-enemy-kind-is-the-authored-unit.md): a composition entry persists its kind **by name, not ordinal**, since making `Enemy_Kind` persisted swapped a union-guessing hazard for a renumbering one.
- [Weapon catalog expansion](issues/08-weapon-catalog-expansion.md): **twelve weapons — three families, four tiers each** — with **tier 0 the only free pick**, amending [ADR-0008](../../docs/adr/0008-weapon-family-is-run-scoped.md). `weapon_family_kinds` was read two ways at once, as the priced ladder and as the Run_Start menu, so Shotgun cost 0 Gold and dead-ended the Shop while Pistol cost 390 to reach the same place; `shop.odin` and the ADR already contradicted each other about this. Climbing a tier buys **a different style, not a bigger number**: each family's top tier answers what its lower tiers cannot — **Ranged buys the crowd** (a piercing **Rifle**, since every Gun bullet `break`s on first hit and Ranged was the only family locked out of rung 4), **Melee buys distance** (**Spear** then **Greatsword**; melee already sweeps uncapped, so its swarm problem was standing in the crowd, not throughput), **Magic buys the boss** (an instant hitscan **Lightning Staff** — the only thing in the game that spawns no travelling projectile, which is what stops a no-area magic bolt being a Pistol). Dagger and Sword keep tiers 0–1 and lose only their placeholder comment; `fire_mode` gets no rule. A pierce is tracked by **shot identity, not target identity** — `Bullet.id` against `Enemy.last_hit_bullet_id` — because `unordered_remove` makes remembered indices name the wrong enemy at swarm density and positions are no better. `reserve_ammo` is declared dead, which leaves the **Ammo pickup a no-op on a third of every drop roll**. Two findings outgrew the catalog: **`Clip_Size`'s Additive shape is wrong** (+333% on the Shotgun's clip today, the only Additive Upgrade whose base varies fivefold), and **melee hit detection is a cone from the player's centre** that has never agreed with the blade being drawn — retired here, machinery graduated to [Weapon hit volumes](issues/12-weapon-hit-volumes.md), with `Melee_Weapon` collapsing to one size field. Amends [Shop and upgrades](../shop-and-upgrades/map.md).
- [Enemy pathing at swarm scale](issues/11-enemy-pathing-at-swarm-scale.md): **one shared flow field flooded from the player, and per-enemy pathfinding is deleted** — `find_path`, `Path`, `MAX_SEARCH_NODES` and `Enemy.path` all go, along with `assign_swarmer_slots`. Up to 256,000 node visits and ~1000 alloc/free pairs a frame at 250 bodies become one ~2600-visit flood, rebuilt **when the player changes cell** rather than on a timer, so the field is exact rather than merely fresh. Inverting the search is the point: an unfilled cell is unreachable, a better answer than a failed path. Keyed by inflation radius, so [Boss model](issues/05-boss-model.md)'s separate boss map survives as a second field. Steering now splits on whether a style *collides*: `Floater` never reads the field, and its straight-line retreat — broken for a Spitter, which reverses into geometry — is correct for a **Gazer**, which shoots from inside walls. **`Swarmer`'s ring slots are deleted rather than fixed**: follow the field to the surround radius, then drift along that contour, which makes both known Swarmer bugs unrepresentable and gives a ring that wraps walls instead of cutting through them. Three things the ticket did not know about: **the one authored map has holes and they are the doorways** (888 untiled cells, `player_start` among them, drawing as the clear colour and walkable only because an absent cell reads as `false`) with 648 reachable cells *outside* the authored rectangle, which [Map layout authoring model](issues/02-map-layout-authoring-model.md)'s already-specified `player_start`-on-floor test fails on today; **`move_actor` scans every tile twice per actor per frame** — 852,000 rect checks a frame at 250, larger than the pathing it survives, fixed for free by the field's cell-indexed solid set and fixing the player too; and **spawns were never reachability-checked**, so a pocket spawn holds [ADR-0017](../../docs/adr/0017-run-outcome-and-map-objective.md)'s Cleared open forever. No hard enemy-enemy collision — a solid crowd is a shield for the shooters behind it — and Separation samples ~8 neighbours instead of degenerating to n^2 in the converging case. [ADR-0025](../../docs/adr/0025-enemies-steer-by-a-shared-flow-field.md).

- [Weapon hit volumes](issues/12-weapon-hit-volumes.md): **a weapon's hit volume is the weapon itself, tested live across the swing** — `enemy_in_melee_arc` and its cone are deleted. The prototype drew the two complaints sharper than the ticket wrote them: a Sword resolves with its blade at **-55 degrees**, one extreme of the cone it has already cleared, so the sweep the player watches is entirely *after* the attack; and the cone measured from the feet anchor while the blade is drawn from the chest pivot **12px above it**, a separation `weapon.odin` defended in a comment as stopping visuals from quietly changing reach — reasoning that inverts once the volume *is* the visual. The volume is an **arbitrary set** of polygons per kind, authored in the glyph's unit space and rigid on its pivot frame, in a parallel table with a test (the `weapon_icon_reach` precedent); the guard and grip carry none. Continuous beat swept because swept re-tells the same lie quietly — but the swept model returns **between consecutive frames**, because a Greatsword crosses its arc in 0.07s and moves its tip **50-90px** on the opening frame, enough for a 16px Mite to be touched by nothing. One damage per swing per body, on its **own** id counter rather than sharing `Bullet.id` — a future weapon switch would give the two independent lifetimes. Three things outgrew the ticket: **the swing's active window was already being reconstructed inside `draw_weapon`** from cooldown arithmetic, so `follow_through_time` carries both fire modes and stops being "purely cosmetic" — a Sword now has a Windup *and* a Follow-through, ending Fire mode's sole-gate rule; **`Arc_Width` is Melee's only family Upgrade slot and it scaled the deleted `arc_degrees`**, repointing to `range` and renamed **Reach**; and the per-kind swing arc lands on `Weapon_Visual`, which **stops being cosmetic-only** and buys a Spear its thrust for free, since an arc near zero is a plant rather than a broken sweep. Enemies get no volumes — no drawn weapon, no lie to fix — only `Melee.attack_range` measured surface to surface, which fixes [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md)'s unreachable Breaker. The cone survives as the flamethrower's own, where it was always honest. Prototype on branch `prototype/weapon-hit-volumes`. [ADR-0026](../../docs/adr/0026-a-weapon-hits-only-what-it-touches.md).

- [Content-scale integration sweep](issues/09-content-scale-integration-sweep.md): **almost nothing breaks that the upstream tickets did not break deliberately — what breaks is the order you land them in**, so the answer is a **six-stage dependency-ordered checklist** rather than a per-system list: the flow-field substrate, then persistence shape, then the roster, then maps and the ladder, then weapons, then boss and presentation. Landing the nine-kind roster before the field would put 150-250 bodies on per-enemy BFS and make the roster look like the mistake. Four seams described the wrong code: the Movement Style visualiser reads separation radii in a fixed purple, not the colour switch (it breaks anyway, on the deleted `swarmer_surround_radius`); the proportional-not-countable trap is on the **Magic** row, not Ranged, which has countable streak counts; `start_new_run` fires *before* Map Selection so it cannot build a field at all — the field and the per-Run collision map both build on entering `Playing`, the one moment a Run and a Map both exist; and the tuning surface at risk is the editor's per-entry sliders, not `debug.odin`, which already has a slider. Also `ENEMY_GROUNDED_COLOR :: rl.RED` while the catalog gives Grounded green and red to Charger, so the repalette must land in the preset rewrite's own commit or the roster reads inverted for a build. Decided: **the ground layer is defined by whose information it carries** — floor patches and Tell zones under the actors, poison clouds staying above, because a cloud is the player's own output rather than the world's; the light wash draws **inside** `draw_world_contents` as a world-space quad, since suppressing it makes the world change colour as a menu opens; the boss's guaranteed drop is a **preset field** read where `maybe_spawn_pickup` already reads the payout, not a call-site branch; **`Pickup_Kind.Ammo` and the entire reserve are deleted** (`reserve_ammo`, `refill_weapon_reserve`, both clip constants, an unreachable empty-weapon indicator state) because `69420` was a decision written as a joke; the five family hues stay **named constants read by the preset table**, so hue-means-family is enforced rather than advised; **two tests, not one** — seven map-validity checks and a separate preset-well-formedness test, which turns [Elite and affix tier](issues/10-elite-and-affix-tier.md)'s inflation-envelope convention into a check; `time_limit` stays authored with `<= 0` still meaning untimed; one `weapon_family_kinds` with a corrected comment and the arrow-key hotkey **retired**, which removes its second reader and a cheat that skips the whole priced ladder; Desert Dungeon patched in the same change that lands the test it currently fails; `Debug_Visualizer.Pathfinding` renamed **`Flow_Field`**; `draw_tilemap` culls, closing [Art revamp](../art-revamp/map.md)'s per-frame-transform fog; [ADR-0018](../../docs/adr/0018-weapon-visual-identity-is-per-kind-not-per-family.md)'s countable rule binds `weapon_visuals` too; one parametric enum-identity-string helper whose **read path errors** rather than falling through to the zero value, since a dropped kind means a rung spawns fewer enemies and Clears anyway; the boss phase rotation lives **inside** the `Attack_Style` variant; and Run_Start keeps three columns for the family labels while losing the `max_kinds` derivation. The editor **keeps a tuning surface** — its own mode, exporting the `enemy_presets` Odin literal rather than gaining a loadable data file, which would undo [ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-enemy-kind-is-the-authored-unit.md) and hand the roster back to the union-guessing marshaller exactly as the boss makes it a nested union: [ADR-0027](../../docs/adr/0027-the-editor-generates-source-it-does-not-gain-a-data-format.md). Three file-placement questions are handed to the implementer by name. Amends [Shop and upgrades](../shop-and-upgrades/map.md).

## Not yet specified

- Whether new enemy or map content wants **new pickup kinds** to drop. **Half of this is now answered**: [Content-scale integration sweep](issues/09-content-scale-integration-sweep.md) deleted `Pickup_Kind.Ammo` and the whole Gun reserve behind it, leaving Gold/Health and a drop roll that is a coin flip. Whether the roster ever *grows* is still fog — now with the rule the deletion established, that the game rolls once per death, so every kind added dilutes every other one.
- **Balance numbers across the whole catalog** — playtesting work, not a design branch, matching how every prior map deferred its numerics. [Enemy catalog](issues/04-enemy-catalog.md) named the exceptions that are *not* free to move: the Breaker's health against the inflation envelope, the Warden's against `ENEMY_SIZE_MAX`, and the Mite's Gold against a per-body drop roll at swarm density. [Weapon catalog expansion](issues/08-weapon-catalog-expansion.md) adds two more: the Lightning Staff's damage against the Warden's ~220 health and a 4.05x `Damage` upgrade ceiling, and the rule that **weapon damage may never be answered by raising enemy health** — `enemy_body_size` derives size from `max_health` below the clamp, so below ~72px health *is* the silhouette. [Weapon hit volumes](issues/12-weapon-hit-volumes.md) adds a third and a caveat: the **Spear's tip width**, the one number whose being wrong reads as broken machinery rather than a weak weapon, since a blade's collider is narrowest exactly where it reaches furthest; and **every melee `range` needs re-tuning rather than renaming**, because the volume moved 12px from the feet to the pivot.
- **Audio** for any new content — untouched here, and already standing fog on two other maps.

## Out of scope

- **Procedural map layout generation** — considered as the answer to [Map layout authoring model](issues/02-map-layout-authoring-model.md) and rejected: it works against a ladder whose rungs are meant to be distinct learnable places, and it would have to re-earn the connectivity and spawn-placement guarantees hand-drawing gives by eye. It remains a coherent future effort if a rung count ever outgrows what a person will draw, but it is a fresh effort, not a resumption of this one. [ADR-0021](../../docs/adr/0021-map-layouts-are-hand-authored-places.md).
- **Endless, prestige or ascension modes** — what happens after all five rungs are cleared, raised while resolving [Map ladder shape](issues/03-map-ladder-shape.md) and ruled past the destination. Rungs stay replayable and the reason to keep playing is Account progression, which is already unbounded. An endless or ascending mode is a fresh effort with its own balance frame, not a rung on this ladder.
- **New Relics, Upgrades, and `Account_Stat`s** — adjacent content axes, considered during scoping and not among the four selected. They spend the same Gold and would each need their own balance frame.
- **UI/menu chrome redesign** — settled by [Menu UI polish](../menu-ui-polish/map.md). Map Selection's *information* needs are in scope (see [Map ladder shape](issues/03-map-ladder-shape.md)); its visual language is not.
- **Implementation of any of it** — decisions only, per the Destination.
