# Ground-targeted casts lock their target at Trigger, not Resolve

Status: accepted

This map locked "aim tracks the mouse live throughout Windup" as a destination-level rule during charting (see CONTEXT.md's Windup entry, and ticket 01's `resolve_weapon_action` reading live `aim_dir`/`mouse_world` at Resolve time): every weapon fires toward wherever the player is aiming the instant Windup completes, not wherever they were aiming at Trigger. That held for every case considered at the time — Gun, Melee_Weapon, and Magic's Fireball/Flamethrower all aim along a live `aim_dir`.

Poison_Cloud breaks the pattern: it doesn't aim along a direction at all, it targets a ground point via `mouse_world`/`cast_range`/`clamp_point_to_range`. The Magic windup prototype built it live-tracking anyway, for consistency with the locked rule — the telegraph ring followed the mouse for the full Windup, and the cloud landed wherever the cursor ended up at Resolve. Tried live: **rejected**. It read wrong.

The distinction: `aim_dir` is a direction the player is continuously adjusting — there's no natural "commit" moment, so tracking it live through Windup is just staying responsive to something already in motion. A ground target is a discrete pick — the player chooses a spot, and the natural mental model (the one ground-targeted abilities use across the genre) is that the choice is locked in once made, with the telegraph marking *that* spot regardless of where the player subsequently moves the mouse or repositions themselves. Tracking it live doesn't read as responsiveness, it reads as the game not having heard the player's input.

Poison_Cloud's target now locks at **Trigger**, not Resolve: `try_use_weapon`'s Semi_Automatic path needs a small variant-aware hook — a no-op for every other spell and weapon type — that captures `mouse_world` into the `Magic` variant the instant Windup starts, for `spell_kind == .Poison_Cloud` specifically. `resolve_weapon_action` then reads that locked point for Poison_Cloud rather than live `mouse_world`, while continuing to read live `aim_dir` for Fireball and Flamethrower exactly as ticket 01 already established. The locked-target field stays on `Magic` (not lifted to the common `Weapon` struct like `windup_fraction`/`follow_through_time` were in ADR-0003) — this isn't a cross-variant concern gated by `Fire_Mode`, it's specific to one spell's targeting model, so it stays where the rest of Poison_Cloud's spell-specific state already lives.

Consequence: "aim tracks live" is no longer a universal rule for every Windup-gated weapon — it holds for anything aiming along `aim_dir`, and is overridden for anything targeting a discrete ground point. Any future ground-targeted ability should default to locking at Trigger, not live-tracking, unless a specific reason argues otherwise.
